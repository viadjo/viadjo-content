The rental market in Amsterdam is scarce, with room prices starting at around €600 per month. Investing in property can be a good alternative for parents with sufficient financial resources.

Savings interest rates are low, while investing in property tends to retain its value over the long term. Additional benefits include providing secure housing for your children and choosing the co-tenants yourself.

Amsterdam is, however, a very difficult and overheated market. Both purchasing a property and complying with local rental regulations present challenges.

ViaDjo can help with the purchase and advise on rental possibilities, including calculating the points system for properties in the private rental sector.
