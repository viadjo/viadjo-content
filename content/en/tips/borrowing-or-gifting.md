There are two ways in which parents can help their children purchase a home.

## Borrowing

As a parent, you receive more interest on the loan you provide than you would typically receive from a savings account. At the same time, your child benefits from a lower net interest rate, because parents can gift part of the amount back tax-free, up to a maximum of €5,677 per year.

## Gifting

Parents may make a one-time tax-free gift of €106,671 to their child. Conditions are that the child must be no older than 35 and that the gift must be used to purchase a home.
