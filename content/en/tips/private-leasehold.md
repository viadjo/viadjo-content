Buyers and estate agents are sometimes unnecessarily wary of purchasing a property with private leasehold. Private leasehold means that the leasehold has not been issued by a government authority.

The Royal Dutch Association of Civil-law Notaries (KNB) and the Dutch Banking Association (NVB) have established protective criteria for both leaseholders and mortgage lenders.

## Two situations

**Contracts from before 2013:** A civil-law notary reviews the agreement against the established criteria and issues a Notarial Opinion. A positive (green) opinion makes standard mortgage financing possible.

**Contracts from after 2013:** No opinion is available for these contracts; banks develop their own policies. Since 1 July 2014, however, a new model has improved financing possibilities when agreements comply with this framework.

So don't be put off, but do consult your advisers thoroughly.
