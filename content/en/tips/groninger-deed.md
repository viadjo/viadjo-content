A Groninger deed means that a house is transferred to the buyer before the purchase price has been paid.

## When is it used?

This structure serves specific purposes, such as tax benefits. There can be tax advantages to a quick transfer, for example lower transfer tax when a property is sold within six months. It is also often used in new-build projects where construction only starts once enough homes have been sold.

## The buyer's position

Upon signing, the buyer acquires both the economic and legal ownership. However, the keys remain with the seller until payment has been made and all conditions have been fulfilled.
