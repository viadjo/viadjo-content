There are several situations in which no transfer tax is due when real estate is transferred in the Netherlands.

## Exemptions

- **Inheritance:** If you inherit real estate from someone, you do not pay transfer tax.

- **After divorce:** Following a divorce, no transfer tax is charged when you become the sole owner of a home that was previously jointly owned.

- **Marriage or registered partnership:** When you marry under a community of property regime or enter into a registered partnership with someone who already owns a property, no transfer tax is due.

- **New-build property:** When purchasing a new-build home rather than an existing property, this tax does not apply.

There is also an exemption for first-time buyers on the housing market.
